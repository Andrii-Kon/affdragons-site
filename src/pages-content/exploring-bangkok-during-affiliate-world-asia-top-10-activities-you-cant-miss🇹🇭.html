Exploring Bangkok during Affiliate World Asia: Top 10 activities you can’t miss🇹🇭

  • December 3, 2024

Attending Affiliate World Asia in Bangkok? Great choice! This vibrant city is not just a hub for networking and business but also a treasure trove of unforgettable experiences. Whether you’re looking to unwind after a productive day or dive into the city’s rich culture, Bangkok has something for everyone. Here’s a curated list of must-visit places and activities to enhance your trip🔥

1. The Grand Palace & Temple of the Emerald Buddha🏰

A visit to Bangkok isn’t complete without exploring its most iconic landmark. The Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew (Temple of the Emerald Buddha) are breathtaking displays of Thai art and architecture.

🔝Pro tip:

  • Arrive early to avoid crowds.
  • Dress modestly: covered shoulders and knees are required, or you’ll need to rent appropriate attire.

2. Rooftop dining with stunning city views🏙️

Bangkok is famous for its rooftop restaurants and bars that offer panoramic cityscapes alongside delectable cuisine. Top picks include:

  • Sky Bar at Lebua State Tower: Featured in The Hangover Part II.
  • Vertigo and Moon Bar: A chic spot for fine dining and cocktails.

Unwind with Thai delicacies, signature cocktails, and mesmerizing views of the city lights.

3. Floating markets🛶

Immerse yourself in the vibrant culture of Thai markets by visiting Damnoen Saduak or Amphawa Floating Market. Shop for fresh fruits, souvenirs, and street food straight from boats.

🔝Pro tip: Book a guided tour for easier access to these unique markets.

4. Chinatown’s culinary delights🍣

Foodies will love Bangkok’s Chinatown. By night, it transforms into a gastronomic paradise with dishes like Peking duck, Tom Yum soup, and traditional Thai desserts. The vibrant lantern-lit streets add to the experience.

5. Thai massage at Wat Pho💆

After a long day of conference sessions, treat yourself to a rejuvenating Thai massage at Wat Pho, the temple famous for its reclining Buddha statue. This traditional therapy is the perfect way to recharge.

6. Khao San Road nightlife🏙️

This buzzing street is a haven for backpackers and thrill-seekers. From exotic street food (like fried scorpions) to lively bars and street performances, Khao San Road promises a night of adventure and fun.

7. Shopping extravaganza🛍️

Bangkok is a shopper’s paradise, catering to every taste and budget. Must-visit spots:

  • IconSiam: A luxury mall featuring international brands and fine dining.
  • Chatuchak Market: The world’s largest market offering everything from clothes to antiques.

8. Chao Phraya river cruise🚤

Explore Bangkok’s beauty from the water on an evening Chao Phraya River cruise. Enjoy gourmet dinners with live music and views of illuminated temples.

Top cruise options:

  • Chao Phraya Princess Cruise: Ideal for romantic dinners.
  • Grand Pearl Cruise: A luxurious experience.
  • Supanniga Cruise: Known for authentic Thai cuisine.

9. Sukhumvit nightlife💃

For late-night entertainment, head to Sukhumvit, home to stylish bars, nightclubs, and karaoke venues.

  • Rooftop Bars:
    • Octave Rooftop Lounge & Bar: Offers stunning 360° views.
    • Above Eleven: A jungle-themed rooftop bar.
  • Nightclubs:
    • Levels Club & Lounge: Known for diverse music and chic ambiance.
    • Sing Sing Theater: A unique club with Chinese-inspired decor and live performances.

Pro tip: Dress smart-casual and bring your ID for entry.

10. Cultural shows🪩

Experience the essence of Thai history and culture through spectacular performances.

  • Siam Niramit: A grand production showcasing Thai mythology and history.
  • Calypso Cabaret Show: A modern take on Thai entertainment featuring talented ladyboy performers.
  • Aksra Theatre Show: Combines traditional Thai puppetry with live music.

Where to watch:

  • Ratchada Theatre: For Siam Niramit.
  • Asiatique The Riverfront: Perfect for a stroll, shopping, and catching the Calypso Show.

🔝Pro tip: Book tickets online to avoid inflated prices and secure the best seats.

Bangkok is a city of contrasts, blending modern sophistication with ancient traditions. While Affiliate World Asia will keep you busy professionally, take time to explore the vibrant culture, cuisine, and nightlife of this mesmerizing metropolis.

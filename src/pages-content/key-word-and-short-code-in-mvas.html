Key word and short code in mVas

  • August 22, 2024

Hey there, Dragons! Ready to up your game in the mVAS vertical? Let’s talk about one of the most powerful tools you can have in your arsenal: SMS Keywords and Short Codes. These are your ticket to creating seamless opt-ins, building massive subscriber lists, and driving conversions like a pro.

Why bother with an keyword for a short code?

In a nutshell, an SMS keyword is a word or phrase that people can text to a short code (a 5 or 6-digit number) to subscribe to your service. Imagine your customer seeing a cool promo—something like, “Text ‘WIN’ to 12345 for a chance to win a new iPhone!” They text in, and boom! They’re subscribed and ready to receive updates, offers, and more.

The perks of SMS keywords for short codes

🔹 Instant opt-ins: Short codes make it super easy for users to subscribe—no long forms, no hassle. Just a quick text, and they’re in.

🔹 Automation for the win: Once someone texts your keyword, you can set up automated responses to welcome them or deliver content instantly. It’s like having a 24/7 assistant working to engage your audience.

🔹 Higher conversion rates: People are more likely to engage with a short code than a long phone number or web form. It’s fast, convenient, and fits perfectly into the mobile-first world we live in.

Why it works for mVAS?

In the mVAS space, where every second counts and engagement is key, short codes and keywords are the foundation of any advertiser’s success. 

Don’t forget to work on keywords optimization and attract more and more subscribers to the paid services of mobile operators 📈

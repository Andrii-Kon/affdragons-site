TOP- 5 places to visit in Budapest during AWE conference

TOP- 5 places to visit in Budapest during AWE conference

  • September 4, 2024

If you’re heading to AWE Budapest, you’re in for an unforgettable experience in one of Europe’s most vibrant cities. While you’re there, why not explore some of the city’s must-see attractions between affiliate world conference sessions? We’ve curated a list of the top 5 spots you absolutely have to check out while you’re in town.

1.Parliament Tour

If you’re in Budapest, visiting the Parliament is an absolute must. As the city’s most iconic landmark, the Hungarian Parliament Building is not only a stunning example of neo-Gothic architecture but also a symbol of the nation’s rich history. You can join a guided tour to explore the opulent interior, including the Grand Stairway, the Dome Hall, and the Hungarian Crown Jewels. Perfect for a quick visit between conference sessions at AWE Budapest!

Price: from $15.99

Location: Kossuth Lajos tér 1-3

2. Buda Castle & Fisherman’s Bastion 

Take a stroll through the historic Buda Castle District, where history comes alive with every step. Start your journey at Buda Castle, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and explore its grand courtyards, museums, and breathtaking views of the city. Then, make your way to the Fisherman’s Bastion, a fairy-tale-like terrace offering panoramic views of the Danube, the Parliament, and the rest of Budapest. This area is perfect for both history buffs and those simply looking to capture stunning photos of the city.

 Price: from $19.99

Location: Szent György tér 2 Széchenyi

3. Thermal Bath 

No trip to Budapest is complete without indulging in one of its famous thermal baths. The Széchenyi Thermal Bath, located in the City Park, is the largest medicinal bath in Europe. With 18 pools, including both indoor and outdoor options, it’s the perfect place to relax and unwind after a day of sightseeing. Whether you want to soak in the warm waters, enjoy a steam bath, or try the sauna, Széchenyi offers a rejuvenating experience steeped in tradition.  After the lively parties at the Affiliate World Conference, Széchenyi is the ultimate spot to relax and recharge.

 Price: from $25.99 Location: Állatkerti krt. 9-11 

4. Ruin Bars in the Jewish Quarter 

For a unique nightlife experience, head to the Jewish Quarter and explore Budapest’s famous ruin bars. These eclectic bars are housed in abandoned buildings and courtyards, creating a vibrant and quirky atmosphere. The most famous of these is Szimpla Kert, where you can enjoy a drink surrounded by mismatched furniture, street art, and live music. It’s a must-visit spot for anyone looking to experience the city’s bohemian side and mingle with locals and travelers alike. Sip a drink at Szimpla Kert, surrounded by street art and live music—a perfect hangout with partners also attending AWE Budapest.

Price: from $9.99

Location: Kazinczy u. 14

5. Leo Rooftop Restaurant

Leo Rooftop Budapest offers a dining experience that captivates many with its welcoming atmosphere and stunning views. The location is a highlight for its breathtaking scenery.

Location: Clark Adam ter 1

Exploring the GEO: France 🇫🇷

  • September 18, 2024

Hey, Dragons! Ready to dive into another juicy GEO? Let’s chat about France—yes, the land of croissants, wine, and… killer opportunities for affiliate marketing! Whether you’re deep into mVAS or just exploring new markets, France is a hot spot you don’t want to miss. Let’s break it down, starting with mobile operator coverage, which is key to making sure your campaigns hit the mark.

Mobile operator coverage 📱

France has a top-tier mobile network, which means your ads are going to reach folks whether they’re sipping coffee in Paris or chilling on the French Riviera. The big players in the game? Orange, SFR, Bouygues Telecom, and Free Mobile. These operators cover the entire country with solid 4G networks and are already rolling out 5G in major cities. 

Statistics

Orange: The largest operator in France, Orange has the most extensive 4G coverage across the country, reaching around 99% of the population. It’s also a leader in 5G deployment, with service already available in major urban areas like Paris, Lyon, and Marseille.

SFR: SFR ranks second in terms of coverage, also providing strong 4G services that cover approximately 99% of the population. SFR is rapidly expanding its 5G network, which is active in key cities such as Nice, Bordeaux, and Lille.

Bouygues Telecom: Known for its competitive pricing and strong network, Bouygues covers around 98% of France with its 4G services. Bouygues is also pushing forward with 5G rollout, focusing on cities like Nantes, Montpellier, and Strasbourg.

Free Mobile: Free Mobile offers broad 4G coverage across the country, reaching about 97% of the population. Despite being newer to the scene, Free is aggressively expanding its 5G services, aiming to compete with the larger players in cities such as Toulouse, Marseille, and Paris.

Best landing pages for Affiliate Marketing in France 🎯

When it comes to converting that sweet French traffic, the right landing page is everything. Here are some of the top-performing types for the French GEO:

  1. Download Content: Whether it’s an app, eBook, or exclusive content, download-based landing pages are killing it in France. People love getting something for free, especially if it’s useful or entertaining. 
  2. Authorization Pages: Want to keep it slick and simple? Authorization pages are where it’s at. These pages ask users to log in or sign up using their existing social media accounts or email. It’s quick, painless, and effective, especially for mVAS offers where seamless entry is key.
  3. Dating: Romance is always in the air in France, making dating offers a hot ticket. Dating landing pages that are visually appealing, straightforward, and promise a bit of excitement can capture the attention of French users. Add in some localization, and you’re good to go.

Best Traffic Sources for the French market 🚀

So, you’ve got your landing pages dialed in—now, where’s that traffic coming from? Let’s talk about the best sources for driving French traffic to your campaigns:

  1. Push/Pop Notifications: Push and pop traffic are fantastic for reaching users directly on their devices. With high engagement rates, especially in the mVAS space, these notifications can deliver instant results. They’re perfect for driving users to download content or engage with authorization pages.
  2. Facebook: Still the king of social ads, Facebook is your go-to for highly targeted campaigns in France. With a solid user base across all demographics, you can customize your ads to hit exactly who you want, whether it’s for dating offers or content downloads.
  3. TikTok: The younger crowd in France is all over TikTok, and it’s a goldmine for affiliate marketing. Whether you’re pushing viral trends or engaging challenges, TikTok’s visual-first platform is ideal for grabbing attention and driving traffic to your landing pages. Just make sure your ads are catchy and authentic, and you’ll see that traffic roll in.
